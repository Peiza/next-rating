# Ideas (not planned)

None of these is planned. Don't build any of them until it is moved into the
Process list in CLAUDE.md.

1. Prediction in the players list: each saved player's predicted rating and
   change arrow next to their name, from stored data, so the whole group
   shows at a glance.
2. Rating chart: the player's round ratings over time with the prediction as
   a line, drawn as inline SVG from the stored round history.
3. Share the prediction: a button that shares a short text through the
   phone's share menu (Web Share API), for example "Name: 998 (−1) at the
   November update".
4. Personal bests and milestones: mark the player's highest rated round and
   when the rating passes a round number such as 950 or 1000.
5. Following update too: when some rounds count only in the following update
   (dated after the report deadline), also show the prediction for that
   update, so both are visible.
